## Evidence policy

Articles cite Amazon, Shopify and Google for their own platform definitions, NIST for generative-AI risk, NRF for dated returns estimates, Baymard for checkout research, and GS1 for product identifiers. Hologrow documentation supports Hologrow-specific statements. Third-party analytics competitors' articles are not used as sources. Profound is referenced here only for the requested audit framework, not as a source in the customer blogs.

NRF's 2025 estimates are labeled as such, not relabeled as 2026 outcomes. Baymard's aggregated cart-abandonment rate is not a universal store target or promised recoverable revenue. All numerical operating examples are explicitly synthetic and their assumptions are stated.

## Validation and limits

Run `node build.mjs && node validate.mjs`. The [machine-readable report](quality-report.json) records the word-count scope, schema checks, arithmetic and cross-article text overlap. It excludes navigation and common editorial notes from similarity analysis. Shared layout, definitions and necessary terminology are expected; the check looks for repeated substantive paragraphs and phrase overlap, not zero shared vocabulary.

No global plagiarism database, AI detector or Google internal classifier was used. Low local textual overlap cannot establish that Google will index, rank or cite an article. Search intent overlaps intentionally within the topic cluster, while each article has a distinct job.

[Google's generative-AI content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) emphasizes accuracy, relevance and added value; mass production without added value can violate spam policy. It does not offer a certificate that text is “undetectable.” [Google's AI-features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says the existing SEO fundamentals apply and special AI markup is not required. FAQPage markup here describes visible FAQs; no rich result is promised.

Before official-domain publication, a Hologrow owner should verify connector capabilities and review product positioning. If these articles are also published on hologrow.ai, choose one canonical destination and update redirects/canonicals and internal links together. Two independently indexed copies should not be the default migration plan.
